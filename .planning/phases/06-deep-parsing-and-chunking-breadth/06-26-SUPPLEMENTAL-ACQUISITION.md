The [wrapper protocol](https://github.com/opendatalab/MinerU/blob/mineru-4.1.6-released/docs/en/usage/file_parse.md) can leave a task running after client timeout and has no wrapper cancellation route. The current adapter refuses `202`; no task polling or upstream cancellation has been verified. Do not claim bounded cancellation until a real service and an operator-approved upstream cancellation path are tested.

Record the failure; do not substitute a newer image, package, model, or tier without a revised checkpoint.
