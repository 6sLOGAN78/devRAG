# Plan 06-24 review

RAPTOR is an opt-in dataset job over a frozen snapshot of existing indexed leaves. It uses the workspace's selected chat model and the dataset embedding model, with a fixed source-only prompt. The pure hierarchy limits the input to 256 leaves, three levels, 64 summaries, eight children per summary, 12,000 context characters and 2,000 summary characters. The service job further caps the snapshot at 200 source chunks. Small inputs use deterministic nearest-neighbor pairs; larger inputs require the approved, pinned UMAP/GMM runtime in isolated DeepDoc. Its cache is placed in writable `/tmp` for the non-root container. Native failure is typed and does not fall back to a claim of success.

Summary IDs derive from the snapshot/config digest, level and children. Every summary stores real child and original leaf IDs, dataset identity and generation. Index insertion precedes job completion; retrieval and saved chat references expose a summary only after the whole job succeeds and only while it is the latest completed generation. Every source document's status, content hash and parse-task provenance and every indexed leaf's enabled/current state are checked on read. An incomplete, cancelled, failed, deleted or changed generation is hidden. Previously stored chat references are rechecked and stale derived text is replaced with an unavailable marker. The citation drawer opens the original leaf rather than a synthetic summary document or box.
